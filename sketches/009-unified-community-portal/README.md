# Variant 009: Unified Community Portal & Cultural Heritage Hub (009-unified-community-portal)

## Design Stance
- **Surface Archetype:** Hybrid Civic Action Platform (Operate + Explore + Cultural Archive).
- **Brand System:** Authentic Syasyah Samaj theme:
  - Brand Primary: Nepal Crimson (`#b91c1c` / `crimson-700`)
  - Grounding Surfaces: Warm paper neutrals (`stone-50` to `stone-100`) and charcoal ink (`stone-900`)
  - Accent Gold/Brass: Traditional heritage badge accents (`#92400e` / `#fef3c7`)
- **Target Audience:** All members of the Syasyah community in Lalitpur (Yala), youth exploring family lineages and Guthi traditions, community elders seeking historical records, and office administrators managing civic services.

## Synthesized Sections (Per User Directive)
1. **कालक्रम (Timeline):**
   - 4-stage chronological milestone cards spanning founding in 2056 BS, 24-Ilaka decentralization in 2062 BS, 2072 BS earthquake relief, and 2080-83 BS digital transformation.
2. **डिजिटल संग्रह (Archives) — सामुदायिक अभिलेख तथा पाण्डुलिपि:**
   - Filterable repository supporting deeds/manuscripts (तमसुक तथा पाण्डुलिपि), historical photography (तस्बिर संग्रह), and Guthi regulations (गुठी आचारसंहिता).
   - Interactive modal preview for scanned documents.
3. **आदरणीय अग्रज (Elders) — समाजका विशिष्ट धरोहर व्यक्तित्वहरू:**
   - Dedicated tribute cards for founding elders, classical Guthi music masters (दाफा भजन), and women's empowerment pioneers.
4. **Civic Action Hub & 24 Ilakas (From 006):**
   - 4-metric community pulse bar (इलाका, सदस्य, गुठी, रक्तदाता).
   - Upcoming guthi & community events timeline with agendas and route maps.
   - Interactive 24-Ilaka directory with direct phone calling.
5. **Citizen Services & Blood Donors (From 008):**
   - Live citizen application status tracker.
   - 1-click interactive blood donor filter matrix (O+, A+, B+, AB+).
   - Direct link to the offline-first accounting SPA (`/app` स्यस्यः धुकू).

## Anti-Slop Audit Checklist
- [x] **No arbitrary AI purple/gradients:** Uses authentic crimson, stone neutrals, and paper tones.
- [x] **No icon topper clichés or generic cards:** All content features realistic Devanagari copy, authentic Newari Guthi references, and verified Lalitpur geography.
- [x] **Interactive state changes:** Master real-time search, archive category filter, blood donor matrix, application status check, and document preview modals.
