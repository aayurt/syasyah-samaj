# Variant 006: Civic Action Hub (006-civic-hub)

## Design Stance
- **Principle:** High-density, utilitarian community operating portal.
- **Target Users:** Samaj members seeking immediate local services, emergency blood donation contacts, Ilaka coordinators, and dues verification.
- **Layout Axis:** Asymmetrical 2-column split (65% dynamic community feed & timeline / 35% sticky member ID actions, executive contacts, and official publications).

## Key Choices
- **Command Search Bar:** Instant real-time filtering across Ilakas, committee representatives, and emergency blood groups.
- **Emergency Blood Matrix:** Dedicated interactive blood donor panel with direct phone access.
- **Prominent Civic Metric Bar:** Quick accountability view showing active Ilakas (24), registered members (1,842), guthi coordination, and donor pool.
- **Strict Grayscale Architecture:** Uses crisp 1px borders, high-contrast typography, and clear button borders rather than color fills.

## Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Day-to-day community utility, rapid emergency lookups, practical civic transactions.
- **Weak at:** Immersive visual storytelling or photographic exhibition.

## Best For
A working community portal where visitors come to get things done (find an Ilaka coordinator, check meeting dates, get official forms, verify dues).
