# Variant 007: Cultural Heritage & Editorial Archive (007-editorial-heritage)

## Design Stance
- **Principle:** Editorial, cultural prestige, preservation of Newar heritage and institutional memory in Yala (Lalitpur).
- **Target Users:** Community elders, researchers, diaspora members, and youth discovering historical documents, Guthi rules, and family lineages.
- **Layout Axis:** Centered editorial column with serif typography, chronological milestones, and an interactive manuscript & photograph archive grid.

## Key Choices
- **Serif Typographic Hierarchy:** Elevates the cultural stature of Syasyah Samaj with classic editorial dignity.
- **Interactive Archive Filter:** 1-click filtering across historical deeds (तमसुक), archival photographs, and Guthi regulations.
- **Historical Milestones (Timeline):** Explicit progression from founding in 2056 BS through 24-Ilaka decentralization, disaster response, and modern digitization.
- **Strict Grayscale Tone:** Honors the archival nature of historical records without garish colors.

## Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Cultural prestige, community pride, deep storytelling, archival exploration.
- **Weak at:** Rapid daily transactions (e.g. paying dues in 2 clicks or checking cashier balances).

## Best For
A flagship public presence that commands respect, educates the community, and safeguards Yala's cultural history.
