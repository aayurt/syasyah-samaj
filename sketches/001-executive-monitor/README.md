# Stance A: Executive Financial Summary (001-executive-monitor)

## Design Stance
- **Audience:** Samaj Executive Committee, Treasurer, and Auditors.
- **Goal:** Clear, high-level financial confidence at a glance. Prioritizes macro health (Cash position, net monthly surplus/deficit, and trial balance sanity) before individual transactions.
- **Layout Axis:** Asymmetric 2-column (70% analytical charts + recent ledger / 30% bank balance breakdown + balance audit pill).

## Key Choices
1. **Prominent 4-metric KPI ribbon:** Total Liquidity (Cash + Bank), Current Month Inflow, Current Month Outflow, Outstanding Member Receivables.
2. **Monthly Comparison Chart:** 6-month visual bar comparison (Income vs Expense in pure CSS).
3. **Audit Sanity Card:** Explicit "Trial Balance = Balanced" verification pill so executives know books are mathematically sound.
4. **Bilingual labels:** Nepali-first with English subtitle hints for dual audit clarity.

## Trade-offs
- Less focused on rapid data entry. The cashier has to click "+ नयाँ भौचर" to open the entry form rather than typing directly on the landing page.
