# Stance C: Member Dues & Guthi Collection Stance (003-community-dues)

## Design Stance
- **Audience:** Membership Committee, Guthi Administrators, and Collection Staff.
- **Goal:** Drive the primary revenue and community function of Syasyah Samaj: membership registration, annual dues collection progress, tracking critical overdue members (90+ days), and dedicated fund allocations (Welfare/Death-benefit, Education, Building).
- **Layout Axis:** Goal-oriented stacked layout (Top: Annual Dues Progress Banner / Middle: Interactive Aging Buckets 0-30, 31-90, 90+ / Bottom: Filterable Member Roster + Samaj Dedicated Funds).

## Key Choices
1. **Fiscal Year Collection Target Bar:** Real-time percentage progress of annual dues collection (e.g. 81% completed).
2. **Interactive Aging Cards:** Clicking 0-30, 31-90, or 90+ immediately filters the member roster below.
3. **Community-first Terminology:** Focuses on wards, localities (च्यासल, मंगलबजार, हौगल), family units, and dedicated community welfare funds (आपतकालीन तथा मृत्यु-संस्कार कोष).
4. **Actionable Roster:** Direct "शुल्क तिर्नुहोस्" (Pay Dues) button next to each member that prefills a receipt.

## Trade-offs
- Less emphasis on double-entry accounting mechanics (Debit/Credit ledgers, P&L bar charts). Treats the accounting as underlying plumbing and surfaces community member relations.
